## Rules for writing form answers

1. Only use facts from the resume below and the Story Bank. Never invent a metric, tool, employer, title or date.
2. If a question asks about something not done, say so plainly and point to the closest real experience.
3. First person, plain and specific: 40–120 words for short fields, under 250 for essays. Avoid clichés ("spearheaded", "leveraged", "passionate about", "results-driven", "seamless").
4. Start cover letters from the base in Story Bank → Cover Letter & About Me.
5. Leave for {name} and flag: {legal and consent questions, references, salary figures not listed above, …}.
6. Never click Submit. The apply step fills the form and stops for review.
